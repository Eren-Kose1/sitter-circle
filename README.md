# SitterCircle

A one-shot, vibe-coded prototype for Week 1 of *GUI Design & Web Optimization* (Cycle 1, personal product).

> **Brief: Babysitting / childcare swap**
> A parent needs a trusted babysitter on short notice, while every option they know is either unavailable or someone they've never actually vetted.

**Live:** https://eren-kose1.github.io/sitter-circle/

## What it does

- **Find a sitter:** choose tonight, tomorrow or another day and a time. Everyone is grouped by how well *you* know them: people who have looked after your kids, people your circle vouches for, and verified strangers. Ask one person, or ask the three most trusted free people at once; the first yes gets the booking and the other requests are withdrawn.
- **My circle:** the same people, explained by trust level.
- **Swap hours:** parents look after each other's kids instead of paying. Hours you sit are credited, hours you use are debited.

It's a static prototype: the people are fictional, their replies are simulated after a few seconds, and bookings and swap hours are saved only in your own browser (localStorage).

## Run it locally

No build step and no dependencies: open `index.html` in a browser.
